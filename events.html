Holiday Bible Camp – Summer 2026

Starting 27th July 2026 | Two Weeks

We are excited to announce our upcoming Holiday Bible Camp, a vibrant, engaging, and spirit-filled experience where children can grow in faith, build confidence, and explore their creativity through hands-on activities.

What's Included

  • Daily worship and interactive Bible teaching
  • Fun sports and outdoor activities
  • Games, group challenges, and creative sessions
  • Practical workshops: baking, candle making, bracelet making, and more

Core Focus

Teaching children about their identity in Christ.

Purpose of the Camp

This Bible camp is designed to help children:

  • Understand who they are in Christ
  • Build a personal relationship with God
  • Grow in confidence, faith, and character
  • Discover and develop creative skills through hands-on learning
  • Learn how to apply both spiritual truths and practical skills in everyday life

We believe in nurturing the whole child — spiritually, creatively, and personally. By combining faith with practical activities, children are given the opportunity to learn, express themselves, and grow in a well-rounded and meaningful way.

This is more than just a holiday activity — it is an intentional space for spiritual growth, creativity, joy, and transformation.

Past

Children's Prayer Conference

13th July 2024

A special and intentional gathering created to help children understand the power and importance of prayer.

What Took Place

Throughout the day, children participated in joyful and engaging activities including games, singing, lessons, and shared meals. Each child received a thoughtfully prepared goodie bag, making the day both meaningful and memorable.

Key Message

Gospel of Matthew 7:7 — "Ask, seek, knock."

The children learnt that prayer is not complicated — it is an open invitation to speak to God, to seek Him, and to trust that He hears them.

Impact

The aim of the day was to create a space where children could not only learn about prayer but feel confident and encouraged to practice it in their everyday lives. It was a time filled with joy, connection, and spiritual growth.

This conference was a true blessing, made even more special by the support of wonderful helpers who contributed their time and care. Prayer is key, and it was beautiful to see young hearts begin to understand that they can freely talk to their Father, knowing that He hears them.

Past

F.A.W.M. Teenage Girls Conference — You Are Uniquely You

1st September 2018 | Fearfully And Wonderfully Made

A special one-day event created to pour into the lives of teenage girls, reminding them of their identity, value, and uniqueness.

Core Message

Inspired by Psalm 139:14 — Every girl is fearfully and wonderfully made — uniquely created with purpose and beauty.

What Took Place

  • Three inspiring guest speakers who shared messages on identity, confidence, and self-worth
  • Two live performances that brought energy and creativity to the day
  • A time of live worship, creating space for reflection and connection with God
  • Fun games and interactive activities that encouraged bonding and joy

Impact

The atmosphere was filled with encouragement, sisterhood, and positivity. It provided a safe and welcoming space for teenage girls to be reminded of who they are and to embrace their individuality with confidence.

The F.A.W.M. Teen Conference was more than just an event — it was a moment of affirmation and empowerment. Many left feeling uplifted, seen, and reassured of their worth. It stands as a meaningful milestone, reflecting a heart to support and nurture young girls in both their personal growth and spiritual journey.

Past

F.A.W.M. Teen Conference — Finding Purpose in God (Virtual)

3rd September 2022 | Virtual Zoom Edition

A purposeful and empowering online gathering designed specifically for teenage girls to discover their identity, understand their God-given purpose, and build a strong foundation in faith.

Core Message

Rooted in Psalm 139:14 — Helping young girls discover their identity and understand their God-given purpose.

Programme Overview

  • Warm welcome and introduction to the vision behind F.A.W.M.
  • Interactive icebreaker activities to encourage participation and connection
  • Teaching sessions centred on purpose, identity, and personal growth
  • Video explainers with guided discussions
  • Inspiring talks from guest speakers: Ms Molara Akinosho and Mrs Funlayo
  • Reflective closing session with Q&A and follow-up engagement

Key Focus Areas

Throughout the conference, the girls were guided through meaningful teachings on:

  • Discovering their personal "why"
  • Running their God-ordained race with purpose
  • Staying connected to God and their faith
  • Understanding keys to success through biblical principles

Special Resource

7-Day Prayer & Purpose Manual — Each participant received a specially designed manual that included:

  • Key teachings and insights from the conference
  • Daily prayers and scriptures for 7 days
  • Reflection exercises and aptitude-based activities
  • Encouragement for ongoing personal and spiritual development

This manual equipped the girls with practical tools to continue their journey of self-discovery and deepen their relationship with God beyond the event.

Impact

This virtual edition was more than just an online session — it was a meaningful experience that encouraged, equipped, and empowered young girls to walk confidently in their identity and purpose. It stands as a powerful reminder that even in a virtual setting, lives can be impacted, hearts can be reached, and purpose can be awakened.
