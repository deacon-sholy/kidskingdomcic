About Us

Supporting children’s growth, wellbeing, and development through structured, high-impact programmes.

Kids Kingdom CIC is a registered community interest company dedicated to creating safe, consistent, and values-led spaces where children can be intentionally supported and developed.

Who We Are

Kids Kingdom CIC is a registered community interest company dedicated to supporting children’s growth, wellbeing, and development through structured, high-impact programmes.

The organisation was established in response to the growing need for safe, consistent, and values-led spaces where children can be supported beyond traditional environments.

Kids Kingdom CIC delivers mentoring, workshops, and community programmes designed to strengthen children’s confidence, identity, and emotional wellbeing.

Our work is driven by a commitment to seeing children grow in confidence, stability, and purpose, equipping them with foundations that support lifelong development.

🎨

Founder Section

Our Founder - Elizabeth Clark

Kids Kingdom CIC was founded by Elizabeth Clark, who has a strong passion for enriching the lives of children and supporting their personal development.

With experience in working with children through mentoring, ministry, and structured programmes, Elizabeth is committed to creating safe and inspiring environments where children can grow in confidence, identity, and wellbeing.

Her vision is to see children supported holistically, developing not only socially and emotionally, but also gaining strong values and a sense of purpose that will guide them in life.

Values & Foundation

Our foundation

Love Identity Purpose Integrity Growth

Kids Kingdom CIC is rooted in Christian values that emphasise love, identity, purpose, integrity, and personal growth.

We believe every child carries inherent value and potential, and deserves to be supported in environments that are safe, consistent, and uplifting.

While our foundation is values-led, our programmes are inclusive and open to all children, designed to serve the wider community without exclusion.

We aim to provide environments where children are encouraged, respected, and equipped to thrive in every area of life.

How We Support Children

Our work is not only about activities. It is about creating the right environment for children to feel safe, seen, and equipped to grow.

At Kids Kingdom CIC, we aim to provide spaces where children are consistently encouraged, positively guided, and given opportunities to develop in confidence and character.

We understand that children thrive when support is intentional. That is why our programmes combine structure, creativity, mentoring, and values-led development in ways that are engaging and meaningful.

Whether through mentoring sessions, workshops, enrichment activities, or community-based programmes, our focus remains the same: helping children build strong inner foundations that support lifelong wellbeing and growth.

Trust & Safety

A Safe & Supportive Environment

At Kids Kingdom CIC, the safety and wellbeing of every child is our highest priority.

All staff and volunteers are DBS-checked and committed to maintaining a safe, secure, and nurturing environment. Sessions are structured, supervised, and delivered with a strong focus on safeguarding, positive behaviour, and emotional wellbeing.

We follow clear safeguarding practices and ensure appropriate staff-to-child ratios at all times, so that every child is properly supported and cared for.

Our aim is to create a space where children feel safe, valued, and confident to grow, while giving parents peace of mind.
