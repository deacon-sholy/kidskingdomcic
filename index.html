About Kids Kingdom CIC

Kids Kingdom CIC exists to enrich the lives of children and young people by providing safe, structured, and empowering programmes that support emotional wellbeing, confidence, character development, leadership skills, and spiritual growth.

We deliver mentoring programmes, including girls' identity and self-worth initiatives, workshops, conferences, daily camp-style programmes, after-school clubs, and holiday activities designed to help children understand their identity, develop resilience, and build strong moral foundations.

Our programmes include faith-informed teaching, values-based education, leadership development, and age-appropriate prayer sessions that promote hope, purpose, and positive life choices.

We are committed to serving children and families within the community, including those from disadvantaged backgrounds, and ensuring our activities are accessible, inclusive, and impactful.

All profits generated are reinvested into expanding programmes, subsidising access for families in need, and increasing long-term community impact.
