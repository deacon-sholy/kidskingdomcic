About Kids Kingdom CIC

Building confident children through care, values, and community.

Kids Kingdom CIC enriches the lives of children and young people with safe, structured programmes that nurture emotional wellbeing, confidence, leadership, character, and spiritual growth.

We blend mentoring, after-school clubs, holiday activities, workshops, and community events to help every child find identity, develop resilience, and make positive choices.
